Our games

ObbLandZ

  • Obby / Tycoon
  • Roblox
  • 20 players

A five-floor glass tower with your own turf plot bolted onto it. Four difficulty arms — Unc, Noob, Buff and PSYCH — spiral out from the hub, a five-level dungeon runs beneath the lobby, and there are eggs, critters, find-hunts and sky races threaded through the whole thing. Every inch of it is generated at runtime.

GO K-Fish!

  • Social / Cards
  • Roblox
  • 40 players

Go Fish, rebuilt as a K-Pop hangout. Nine tables, up to six players each, played with photocards of real group members. Six themed tables reroll their rules every thirty minutes, three stay traditional, and an NPC will sit down with you if the room is quiet — so there is always a game running.

The Tail of Castroid

  • Metroidvania
  • Roblox
  • Co-op

Castlevania and Metroid, played on a Zelda-style grid where the camera snaps room to room. Nine themed regions, each with sub-areas and a boss dungeon; rooms switch between side-on, top-down and a spinnable 2.5D view. Twenty-seven themed tools double as weapons and keys, and parties travel together — with NPC teammates when your friends are offline.

The studio

Small team. Deep systems.

We are a tiny independent studio, which means we get to be stubborn about the parts that matter: worlds built out of real systems rather than set dressing, multiplayer that works whether you brought friends or not, and controls that feel right on a phone as well as a desktop.

Everything we ship is designed to keep unfolding — generated geography, rotating rulesets, collectibles and progression that reward the people who keep coming back.
